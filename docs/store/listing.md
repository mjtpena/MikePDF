## Description
Quire is a fast, private PDF reader for Windows, built to keep untrusted documents away from your files.

Every PDF is opened inside a locked-down sandbox with no access to your files, your network or other apps. If a damaged or malicious document crashes the renderer, Quire recovers and carries on, and the rest of your system is never touched.

Quire has no account, no ads, no upsell and no telemetry. It never connects to the internet. Your documents stay on your PC. It's also open source, so you can read exactly how it works.

**This is an early preview.** It's for reading: annotations, form filling, printing and saving aren't in yet, and they're being worked on in the open.

## What's in this preview ("Product features", one per line)
- Each PDF opens in its own sandbox, with no access to your files or network
- Smooth continuous scrolling, even in very long documents
- Zoom from 10% to 6400%, fit to width or page, and rotate the view
- Thumbnails, bookmarks and internal links for getting around
- Select and copy text, and find words in the document
- Open password-protected PDFs
- Works with screen readers and the keyboard
- Light, dark and high-contrast themes that follow Windows
- Picks up where you left off in each document
- No account, no ads, no telemetry, and no internet connection needed
- Native on Windows 11, on both x64 and ARM64
- Open source under the Apache 2.0 licence
